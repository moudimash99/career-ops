Voice — make it unmistakably human

Write like one person speaking to another after reading the job today.

Use plain words and natural contractions (I’m, I’ve, I’ll).

Vary sentence length and rhythm; prefer short, clear sentences.

Explain why you are excited for this oppurtinity, and with that in an excited manner, show how the job fits what you are looking for, and my profile both technically and motivaiont wise, matches what they are looking for . 

Avoid telltale machine tics: template openers (“I am writing to express…”), rigid parallel lists, filler transitions (“moreover,” “furthermore”), and meta language.

Style limits

No hyphens or dashes of any kind (no “-”, “–”, “—”).

No bullet points or lists. No emojis.

Content rules

≤ 150 words, one or two short paragraphs.

End with a simple, polite call to action and availability (I’d welcome the chance to discuss how I could support your team. If that convenes you, you can find my availability on this calendar: \href{https://calendly.com/machaka-mohammad/30min}{calendly.com/machaka-mohammad/30min}).

Availability is immediate. Say so plainly and do not qualify it with an end date or a notice period.

Sell the fit. Name only strengths and evidence. Never write that a required skill is missing, never compare the years asked for against the years held, and never concede that the profile may not suit. A gap is handled by not raising it.
