# Writing Samples

Drop examples of your professional writing here. The more variety, the more accurately your voice can be captured.

## Why this directory being empty costs you something

`voice-dna.md` and `lib/voice-check.mjs` between them strip the tells of
machine-written text: banned vocabulary, em dashes, the "not X, but Y"
construction, even sentence rhythm. That is a real guardrail and it runs on
every piece of generated prose.

It cannot make writing sound like **you**. Nothing can, without a sample of
how you write. With this directory empty, every cover letter and form answer
lands on voice-dna's generic register: clean, direct, not obviously AI, and
not recognisably yours either. `node lib/voice-check.mjs` says so on every run
until a sample exists.

One file is enough to change that. A cover letter you actually sent, a
LinkedIn About section, a long message you were happy with.

## What counts as a writing sample

Anything you've written that sounds like you:

- Emails or messages you were happy with
- LinkedIn posts or articles
- Cover letters from past applications
- Blog posts or case studies
- Slack or forum posts
- Notes, memos, or internal docs
- Social media posts
- Anything else in your own words

Plain text (`.txt`) and Markdown (`.md`) are preferred — they require no extraction and are read directly. Other formats like `.pdf` or `.docx` work too.

## After adding samples

Tell Claude Code: "recalibrate my writing style" — it will re-scan the files and update your profile.
